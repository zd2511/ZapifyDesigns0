FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FAQs ♡

Don't see your question? Just ask — I'm happy to explain.

What does Zapify Designs offer?

Web design, custom websites, business sites, e-commerce, digital scrapbooks and ongoing website help.

Do I need to have a domain and hosting already?

No. I can guide you through choosing a domain and getting hosting set up.

What is a domain?

Your domain is your website address, like yourbusiness.co.za.

What is hosting?

Hosting is where the files that make up your website live so people can access it online.

How long does a website take to build?

It depends on the size and features of the project. We'll discuss a realistic timeline before starting.

Can I request changes to my website?

Yes. Reviews and revisions are part of the process so the finished site feels right.

Do you create websites for mobile phones?

Yes. Websites are designed to be responsive across phones, tablets and desktops.

Can you create an online store?

Yes. E-commerce projects can include product pages, categories and customer-friendly layouts.

Can you help me after my website goes live?

Yes. Website maintenance and updates can be arranged when you need them.

Do I need to provide the content and images?

You can provide them, or we can discuss what content is needed and how to source suitable visuals.
