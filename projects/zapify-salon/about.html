ABOUT ZAPIFY SALON

More Than Just Hair.

A calm, premium salon concept where great technique meets personal attention and an experience worth returning to.

OUR STORY

Beauty with intention.

Zapify Salon is a fictional demo created to show how a modern hair and beauty business could present its brand online. The design balances editorial typography, warm neutrals, strong photography and a friction-free booking journey.

Every touchpoint is built to feel polished while keeping the important information easy to find.

THE EXPERIENCE

Personal. Relaxed. Precise.

From consultation to final styling, every service is imagined around the individual client. The website can be expanded with stylist profiles, real-time availability, loyalty programmes and online payments.
