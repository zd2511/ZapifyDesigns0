If you contact Zapify Designs, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, project details and messages. We only ask for information that helps us communicate with you about your enquiry or project.

This website may use essential browser storage or small cookies to remember preferences, such as whether you have responded to the cookie notice. The website's enquiry form opens your email application rather than silently storing your message in a website database. When you contact us by email, WhatsApp or another service, that service may process information according to its own privacy policy.

Some website features may link to or integrate with services such as WhatsApp, social networks, hosting providers, maps or payment providers. Those services operate independently and may have their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate a requested service, comply with a legal obligation, protect the website, or complete a project you have asked us to perform.

7. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices

You can choose what information to provide when contacting us. You can also ask us about information we hold about your enquiry, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification requirements.
